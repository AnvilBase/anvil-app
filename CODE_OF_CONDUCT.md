# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a straightforward, harassment-free experience for everyone,
whatever your age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Things that make this project better:

- Being kind and taking other people's perspectives seriously
- Giving and accepting feedback about the work rather than the person
- Owning mistakes, apologising, and learning from them
- Deciding what's best for the project and its users, not just for yourself

Things that aren't welcome:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwanted sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission
- Anything else you'd reasonably expect to be out of place in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, and commit messages — and
whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub](https://github.com/AnvilBase/anvil-app) — open a private security advisory if you need a
confidential channel, or contact a maintainer directly. Reports are reviewed promptly and handled in
confidence.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and will take action they judge fair and
proportionate, which may mean editing or removing a contribution, a warning, a temporary block, or a
permanent ban. Maintainers who don't follow the code of conduct in good faith can expect the same
consequences as anyone else.
